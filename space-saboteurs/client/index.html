How to Play

Move with your hand — hold your open palm up to the camera and drift it around a neutral zone to steer. Crewmates complete tasks and look for trouble. One or more Imposters kill in secret and sabotage the station. Report bodies or call meetings to vote someone out. Crewmates win by finishing all tasks or ejecting every Imposter; Imposters win by evening the numbers or letting a critical sabotage run out.
